About XEC

XEC (X Earnings Calculator) is a free tool that estimates how much a creator could earn from X's creator payouts, and when the next payout lands.

What XEC does

You enter your account name, followers, posts and impressions for a period. XEC turns them into an estimated payout for that period, a projection for the next two-week payout cycle, monthly and yearly projections, a payout calendar, and a shareable earnings ticket you can save or post.

How the estimate works

XEC uses a simulation rate of $0.08 – $0.35 per 1,000 impressions. Your follower count decides where your account sits inside that range: smaller accounts start near the low end and larger accounts move toward the top.

X does not publish an official rate, and real payouts depend on factors XEC cannot see, such as how many of your viewers are verified Premium users. Treat every number as an estimate, not a promise.

Payout schedule

X pays eligible creators every two weeks, with a minimum payout of $30; smaller amounts roll over to the next cycle. XEC's payout calendar marks these dates so you can see what is coming. Program rules can change, so check X's official program page for the latest terms.

Independent and free

XEC is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to X Corp. Your actual earnings appear only in Creator Studio on your own X account.
