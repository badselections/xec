X Original Content Rewards Explained: Requirements, Payouts and What Changed

Updated November 16, 2026 · 7 min read

In 2026 X replaced its Creator Revenue Sharing program with a new one called Original Content Rewards. If you used to earn from ads shown in your replies, or you are trying to get paid on X for the first time, the rules are different now. This guide walks through what the new program pays for, who can join, and the mistakes that get accounts removed.

What changed in 2026

X stopped taking new sign-ups for Creator Revenue Sharing on August 7, 2026. Members who were already enrolled kept earning until September 7, 2026, and from September 8, 2026 X began opening the new program to them. New creators apply through Creator Studio once they meet the requirements.

The biggest shift is what you get paid for. The old program shared revenue from ads shown in the replies under your posts. The new one pays on qualified impressions of your own original posts in the Home Timeline. In short: X now rewards original posts that verified users actually see, not reply threads.

Eligibility requirements

According to X's Original Content Rewards help page, you need all of the following when you apply:

  • Premium: an active X Premium, Premium+ or Premium Business subscription. Basic is not enough.
  • Impressions: at least 500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days. Impressions on your replies do not count.
  • Followers: at least 500 verified followers.
  • Age and location: 18 or older and based in a supported country.
  • Account: a personal or business account in good standing. Political and government organisations are excluded.
  • Content: you are actively posting original content.

X says it reviews applications within about three business days. If you are turned down you can appeal once, and if the appeal fails you can reapply after 90 days.

What counts as a qualified impression

Not every view is paid. A qualified impression is a unique view from a Premium-tier user, in the Home Timeline, where at least half of the post is visible. X excludes:

  • repeat views of the same post by the same account,
  • paid or promoted impressions,
  • artificial or fraudulent impressions (bots, view farms, engagement groups).

This is why two accounts with the same total impressions can earn very different amounts. The share of your audience that is verified, and how many of your views are original posts rather than replies, matters more than the headline number. It is also why any calculator, including XEC, can only give an estimate.

How and when you get paid

  • Every two weeks, as long as your account and posts stay within the rules.
  • $30 minimum. If a cycle earns less, it carries over until you pass the threshold.
  • Payout method: a Stripe payout account, or an X Money account where available. You also complete identity verification with Stripe.

X does not publish a fixed rate per 1,000 impressions. If you want to know what a payout might look like for your numbers, see our guide on how much X pays per 1,000 impressions.

What counts as original content

X wants posts you made yourself: writing, video, design or analysis that carries your own voice or expertise. Commentary counts. Adding context to the news or giving a real take on a trending moment is fine, as long as your contribution is the main value of the post.

What gets posts or accounts excluded

  • Reuploads: content downloaded from X or another platform and posted again, or reposted across platforms by someone who is not the creator.
  • Light edits: changing a word, adding a filter or text overlay, or speeding a clip up does not make it yours.
  • Pure aggregation: compilations of other people's work without real new framing.
  • Automation: content created or posted by automated means, and bots or tools that inflate engagement.
  • Engagement bait: repeatedly asking people to like, reply, follow or repost.
  • Monetization-only content: posts that are only about payouts or monetization coaching.
  • Misleading posts, posts with a helpful Community Note, and anything breaking X's rules or copyright policy.

Since March 3, 2026, posting AI-generated videos of armed conflict without labelling them as AI also triggers a 90-day suspension from payouts, and repeat cases can end payments for good.

A practical checklist before you apply

  • Check your verified impressions and verified followers in Creator Studio, not just total numbers.
  • Make sure most of your reach comes from original posts, not replies.
  • Remove auto-reposting or auto-reply features from any tools you use.
  • Set up Stripe early so identity checks don't delay your first payout.

Quick answers

Can I still join Creator Revenue Sharing?

No. X closed new enrollments on August 7, 2026 and retired the program on September 7, 2026. Original Content Rewards is the program to apply for now.

Do reply impressions count toward the 500,000?

No. X counts Home Timeline impressions from verified users on your posts. Reply impressions are excluded.

What is the minimum payout?

$30. Smaller amounts roll over to the next two-week cycle.

Does X Premium Basic qualify?

No. You need Premium, Premium+ or Premium Business.
