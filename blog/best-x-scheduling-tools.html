Growing on X is mostly about writing good posts consistently. The right tool won't write for you, but it does make it much easier to plan your week, post at the right time and learn what works. These are the four tools creators mention most, and who each one suits.

TypefullyClean writing and scheduling for X
  • Distraction-free editor built for posts and threads
  • Schedule to your best time slots and see a preview before posting
  • Analytics to see which posts performed
  • Easy to share drafts with an editor or teammate

Best for: creators who mainly write, and want a calm place to draft and schedule.

HypefuryScheduling built around a posting queue
  • Queue-based scheduling: fill your slots once a week
  • Recycle your best-performing posts on a schedule
  • Inspiration and post templates to beat a blank page
  • Supports other platforms as well as X

Best for: creators who want a set-and-forget weekly routine.

  • Large searchable library of high-performing posts by niche
  • AI help to rewrite and improve your own drafts
  • Scheduling and analytics in the same place
  • Tools for building relationships with your audience

Using tools safely with Original Content Rewards

X's program excludes content created or posted by automated means and anything that artificially inflates engagement. Scheduling posts you wrote yourself is a normal part of creator work, but if you are in or applying to the program:

  • turn off auto-generated posts, auto-retweets, auto-plugs and auto-replies,
  • use AI features only to edit your own drafts, not to produce whole posts,
  • never use tools that buy or trade engagement.

When in doubt, read X's Original Content Rewards help page.
