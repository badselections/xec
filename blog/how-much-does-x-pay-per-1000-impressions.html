How Much Does X Pay per 1,000 Impressions? Realistic Estimates for 2026

It is the first question every creator asks: how much does X pay per 1,000 views? The honest answer is that X does not publish a fixed rate. Payouts under the Original Content Rewards program depend on which impressions you get, not only how many. Still, you can get a useful range, and that is what this guide is about.

Why there is no single number

X pays on qualified impressions: unique Home Timeline views of your original posts by Premium-tier users, where at least half the post is on screen. Repeat views, promoted views and fake views are removed. So your rate depends on:

  • Verified share: how much of your audience pays for Premium.
  • Original vs. replies: views on replies don't count, so a reply-heavy account earns less per total impression.
  • Audience and timing: advertiser demand varies by region and season, which affects the pool X pays from.
  • Account quality: accounts with engagement bait, reuploads or bot activity can lose payouts completely.

The range XEC uses

XEC models payouts at $0.08 to $0.35 per 1,000 impressions. Your follower count sets where you sit inside that range: around 1,000 followers sits at the bottom, and around 1,000,000 followers reaches the top. Bigger accounts tend to have a larger verified share and more original-post reach, which is why they usually earn more per view.

Treat it as a planning range, not a promise. Your Creator Studio dashboard is the only place that shows what X will actually pay you.

How to raise your earnings per 1,000

  • Post more originals, fewer replies. Replies build relationships, but your paid views come from your own posts.
  • Write for people who pay for Premium. Niche, expert topics like tech, finance, business and AI tend to attract a more verified audience than broad entertainment.
  • Be consistent. A steady schedule keeps your posts in followers' timelines. A scheduler such as Typefully or Hypefury lets you write in batches and post at your best times.
  • Make posts people stop for. A clear first line, a strong image and native video all hold attention. Simple visuals made in Canva are an easy start.
  • Avoid what X removes: engagement bait, reuploads and automated posting. One flagged pattern can cost more than a month of growth.

Turning impressions into a plan

Work backwards from a goal. To reach $300 a cycle at $0.15 per 1,000, you need about 2,000,000 qualified impressions every 14 days. That is roughly 143,000 a day. If you average 20,000 views per post, that means about seven strong original posts a day. Put your own numbers into the XEC calculator to see your range and your next payout date.

Quick answers

Does X pay per view or per engagement?

Under Original Content Rewards, payouts are based on qualified impressions, meaning unique Home Timeline views of your original posts by Premium-tier users.

Why did my payout drop even though my views went up?

Usually because more of your views came from replies, non-verified users or repeat viewers, none of which count. Changes in advertiser demand can also move the pool.

Is $0.35 per 1,000 the maximum?

It is the top of XEC's estimate range, not an official cap. X does not publish a rate.
