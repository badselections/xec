Is X Premium Worth It for Creators? A Simple Break-Even Calculation

Updated November 16, 2026 · 5 min read

Some links in this article are affiliate links. If you buy through them, XEC may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend tools that fit what the article is about.

You can't earn from X's Original Content Rewards without a paid plan: Premium, Premium+ or Premium Business. Basic doesn't qualify. So the real question isn't whether to pay. It's when the subscription pays for itself. Here is a quick way to find out.

The break-even formula

Subscription prices differ by country and change over time, so start with what you pay. Then:

Break-even impressions per month = monthly price ÷ rate per 1,000 × 1,000

For example, if your plan cost $8 a month and your account earned $0.15 per 1,000 qualified impressions, you'd need about 53,000 qualified impressions a month to cover it. At XEC's low-end rate of $0.08 that rises to about 100,000.

Remember the program's own entry bar: 500,000 verified impressions in 90 days, or about 167,000 a month. If you are eligible, you are usually already well past break-even.

When Premium makes sense

  • You're within reach of the requirements: 500 verified followers, and verified impressions growing toward 500K per 90 days.
  • You post daily and treat X as a channel, not a hobby.
  • You also use the non-payout perks, such as longer posts, editing posts and a reply boost, to grow faster.

When to wait

  • You post a few times a week and get under 50K impressions a month.
  • Most of your reach comes from replies, which don't count toward payouts.

Make the subscription work harder

If you pay for Premium, get the most out of it. Batch-write and schedule your week with Typefully so you never miss a day, and design clean visuals in Canva so your posts stand out in the timeline. The more consistent you are, the faster the subscription pays for itself.

Quick answers

Can I earn from X with Premium Basic?

No. Original Content Rewards requires Premium, Premium+ or Premium Business.

Do I need Premium+ to earn more?

Not according to the published requirements. Premium, Premium+ and Premium Business all qualify. Your payout depends on qualified impressions, not your tier.
