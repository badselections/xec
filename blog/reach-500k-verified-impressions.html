How to Reach 500K Verified Impressions on X in 90 Days

Updated November 16, 2026 · 8 min read

Some links in this article are affiliate links. If you buy through them, XEC may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend tools that fit what the article is about.

To join X's Original Content Rewards you need 500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users in 90 days, plus 500 verified followers and Premium. That works out to roughly 5,600 verified impressions a day. It is very reachable for a consistent creator, but only if you grow the right kind of reach. Here is a plan that works within the rules.

1. Pick a lane verified users care about

Because only verified viewers count, your topic matters. Accounts about AI and tech, business, finance, sports analysis, gaming news and design tend to attract more Premium users than general memes. Choose one or two topics you can post about every day for three months, and put them clearly in your bio.

2. Post originals every day

  • Daily target: 3–6 original posts, at least one of them a longer post or thread.
  • Formats that travel: short takes on today's news in your niche, lists ("7 tools I use for…"), before-and-after results, and native video under a minute.
  • Hooks: the first line decides whether people stop. Lead with the result or the surprise, not the background.

Writing in batches is the easiest way to stay consistent. Typefully is built for drafting posts and threads and scheduling them. Hypefury adds queue-based scheduling and recycling of your best posts. Only use features that publish posts you wrote yourself; see the warning below.

3. Use replies to grow, not to earn

Reply impressions don't count toward the 500K, but thoughtful replies on bigger accounts in your niche are still the fastest way to get new followers. Aim for 10–20 genuine replies a day that add information or a real opinion. Those followers then see your original posts, and that is where the counted impressions come from.

4. Learn from what already works

Study the posts that performed best in your niche over the last month: their length, format and the time they went out. Tweet Hunter has a large searchable library of high-performing posts plus writing and scheduling tools, which makes this research much faster. Use it for ideas and structure, then write in your own words. Copying someone else's post is the quickest way to lose eligibility.

5. Make it visual

Posts with a clear image, chart or short video hold attention longer, and X only counts a view when at least half the post is on screen. Simple branded templates in Canva let you turn a tip into a clean image in a couple of minutes.

6. Track it weekly

Check Creator Studio once a week for verified impressions and verified followers. Keep what grows, drop what doesn't. A simple pace check: by day 30 you want about 170K, and by day 60 about 335K. Use the XEC calculator to see what your current pace would pay once you are in.

What to avoid (it can cost you the program)

  • Engagement bait such as "like and repost if…" or follow-for-follow trains.
  • Bots, engagement pods or paid views; X removes these impressions and can ban the account.
  • Automated content: X excludes content created or posted by automated means. Scheduling posts you wrote yourself is normal, but turn off auto-generated posts, auto-retweets and auto-replies in any tool you use, and read X's Original Content Rewards help page if you're unsure.
  • Reuploading other people's clips, even with a caption on top.

Quick answers

How many impressions a day do I need?

About 5,600 verified Home Timeline impressions a day on average for 90 days.

Do impressions from non-verified users count?

Not toward the 500K requirement or toward payouts. Only verified (Premium-tier) viewers count.

Can I use a scheduling tool?

Scheduling posts you wrote yourself is common. Avoid features that generate or repost content automatically, because X excludes automated content.
