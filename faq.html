Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about XEC and X creator payouts.

Is XEC an official X tool?

No. XEC is an independent calculator and is not affiliated with X Corp. Your real earnings are shown only in Creator Studio on your X account.

How does XEC calculate my earnings?

It multiplies your impressions by a simulation rate of $0.08 – $0.35 per 1,000 impressions. Your follower count sets where you fall in that range: about 1,000 followers sits at the low end and about 1,000,000 at the top. The expected range shown under your result uses the lowest and highest rate.

Why is my real payout different from the estimate?

X does not publish a fixed rate. Payouts depend on things XEC cannot see, such as how many of your viewers are verified Premium users, which posts count, and changes to the program. Use XEC as a guide, not a guarantee.

Who is eligible for X's creator payouts?

According to X's help center, creators need an active X Premium, Premium+ or Premium Business subscription, at least 500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days (replies don't count), and at least 500 verified followers. Rules can change, so check X's official program page for the latest terms.

How often does X pay creators?

Every two weeks, while you keep meeting the program requirements. The minimum payout is $30; if a cycle earns less, it rolls over to the next one.

Which impressions should I enter?

Enter the impressions from your X analytics for the same period you select in XEC (7, 14 or 28 days, or 3 months). 14 days matches one payout cycle, so it gives the closest estimate for your next payout.

Do you store my data or my photo?

No. Every calculation, the earnings ticket and any photo you add are processed in your own browser. Nothing you type or upload is sent to or stored on our servers.

How do I save or share my earnings ticket?

Tap Submit after calculating, then use Save to Photos, Copy image or Share on X. On a phone you can also press and hold the ticket and choose Save to Photos. The QR code on the ticket opens xec.life, so anyone who sees your ticket can check their own earnings.

Is XEC free?

Yes. XEC is free to use, with no sign-up.
