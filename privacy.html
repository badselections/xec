Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Summary

XEC does not ask you to create an account and does not collect the numbers, names or photos you enter. Calculations happen in your browser.

Information you enter

The account name, follower count, post count, impressions and any profile photo you add are used only inside your browser to show your estimate and build your earnings ticket. They are not sent to or stored on our servers. When you close or refresh the page, they are gone unless you save the ticket image yourself.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Vercel) may log basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular; it does not use cookies and reports only aggregated statistics. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when your browser requests them.
